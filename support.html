COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Quick Help
How can I view my appointments?

Open Appointments from the sidebar to view your upcoming visits, appointment information and available care details.

Where can I view my medications?

Select Medications from the sidebar to review your medication information and schedule.

Where can I check my vitals?

Open Vitals History to review the health information recorded in your patient portal.

Where can I find my reminders?

Open Reminders to review your upcoming care reminders and scheduled activities.

How can I update my profile?

Open Profile to review the personal information available in your patient portal.

Where can I manage my settings?

Open Settings to manage the available portal, notification, appearance and privacy options.
